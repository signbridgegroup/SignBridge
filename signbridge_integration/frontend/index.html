About the platform

A learning assistant built for deaf and hard-of-hearing students

SignBridge combines AI-powered educational answers with sign-language recognition, giving deaf and hard-of-hearing students direct, understandable access to their course material.

Who we are

We are students from the University of Jordan developing SignBridge as a research-driven educational technology project focused on accessibility, inclusion, and practical AI.

01

Source-backed answers

Sign-language visual presentation

A 3D avatar presents whatever sign motion is available for the answer.
